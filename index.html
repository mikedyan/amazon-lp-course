1994 — The Garage

Where it all began

Jeff Bezos leaves D.E. Shaw to start an online bookstore from his Bellevue garage. The founding principle is already clear: start with the customer and work backwards. There are no formal "leadership principles" yet — just a relentless obsession with what customers want.

💡 Key Insight: "Day 1" means the company should always behave like a startup — hungry, customer-focused, and willing to experiment. "Day 2 is stasis. Followed by irrelevance. Followed by excruciating, painful decline." — Jeff Bezos
